Start the free helper on your computer, then leave this page open. It refreshes by itself.
  1. Open your nba-live-board-main folder (the one with relay.py).
  2. Right-click an empty space in the folder → Open in Terminal.

  4. If Chrome asks to allow access to devices on your local network, click Allow.

Live suggestions · 2.00+ only

Stake = quarter-Kelly, capped at 2% of bankroll. Signals in the last 4 minutes are skipped because prices jump too fast to act on. Always check your bookie's price first.

Signal log

    How it works. Every 4 seconds the board reads ESPN's live NBA feed (score, clock, starters, fouls, injuries, bookmaker odds) and Polymarket's live prices. The model starts from the pre-game market price, then updates the win chance for the score, the time left and starters fouling out. When a side priced at 2.00 or more has a model chance high enough that chance × odds − 1 beats your minimum edge, it shows as VALUE.
    Honest limits. Market odds are Polymarket's, not your bookie's; type your bookie's price in "Your odds" to use it instead. The model is simple and the market usually reacts within seconds, so most live signals are small and short-lived. Nothing here is a guarantee.
